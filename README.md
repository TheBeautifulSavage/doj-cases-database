# DOJ Cases Database — Department of Justice Prosecutions

> Tracking federal crime news, DOJ prosecutions, and US Attorney cases from all 50 states.

## About

The Department of Justice prosecutes tens of thousands of federal criminal cases annually across all 50 states and 94 federal judicial districts. This database tracks DOJ cases, US Attorney press releases, and federal court outcomes nationwide.

## The Department of Justice Structure

The DOJ prosecutorial network consists of:

- **US Attorneys**: 94 presidentially appointed US Attorneys leading prosecution in federal districts
- **DOJ Main Justice Divisions**: Criminal Division, National Security Division, Civil Rights Division, and others with specialized prosecution authority
- **FBI**: Primary investigative agency for federal crime
- **DEA**: Drug Enforcement Administration
- **ATF**: Bureau of Alcohol, Tobacco, Firearms and Explosives
- **IRS-CI**: IRS Criminal Investigation for financial crimes
- **HSI**: Homeland Security Investigations

## Major DOJ Case Categories

### Organized Crime
RICO (Racketeer Influenced and Corrupt Organizations) prosecutions target criminal enterprises from street gangs to sophisticated organized crime networks. DOJ Organized Crime Strike Forces operate in major cities nationwide.

### National Security
Terrorism, espionage, and export control violations are prosecuted by the National Security Division. These cases often involve classified evidence and specialized procedures.

### Civil Rights
Federal civil rights violations, police misconduct, and hate crimes are prosecuted by the Civil Rights Division.

### Environmental Crime
Environmental crimes, including illegal dumping, Clean Water Act violations, and pollution crimes, are prosecuted by the Environment and Natural Resources Division.

### Public Corruption
The Public Integrity Section prosecutes corruption by public officials at all levels of government.

## DOJ Press Release Structure

DOJ press releases follow a standard format:
- Date and district identification
- Names of defendants
- Charges filed or conviction/sentencing details
- Agency attribution (FBI, DEA, ATF, etc.)
- Statement from US Attorney

Grimytimes.com parses and organizes these press releases for research and public information purposes.

## Federal Judicial Districts

The 94 federal judicial districts are organized into 12 regional circuits plus the Federal Circuit. Each district has its own US Attorney's office responsible for federal prosecution in that geographic area.

- **Northern/Southern/Eastern/Western** district designations for large states
- Smaller states may have a single federal district
- Each district has distinct prosecution priorities and caseloads
